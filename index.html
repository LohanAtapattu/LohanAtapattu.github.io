01 — ABOUT

Circuits for
human signals.

My research interests are neural interfaces, low-power biomedical ICs, and implantable electronics.

At the University of Moratuwa, I worked on a dry-contact Ear-EEG system and an analog IC for electrode–skin impedance monitoring. I’m currently exploring low-noise CMOS amplification for neural recording.

I’m interested in the full path from electrode and analog front end to reliable biosignal data, especially for wearable and implantable systems.
