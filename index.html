01 — A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Engineering at
the edge of biology.

I’m interested in the tiny, thoughtful systems that help technology connect with human physiology.

My work brings together low-power analog and mixed-signal circuits, biosignal acquisition, and digital signal processing. At the University of Moratuwa, I contributed to biomedical IC research and undergraduate tapeout projects, including a dry-contact Ear-EEG system designed to monitor electrode–skin impedance and help cancel motion artifacts.

I’ve also worked on FPGA-based communications, assistive devices, and engineering education. Across each project, I enjoy moving between circuit-level details and the real-world needs a system should serve.
