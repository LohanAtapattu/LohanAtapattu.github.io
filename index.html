01 — ABOUT

Engineering at
the edge of biology.

I’m interested in the small, thoughtful systems that help technology connect with human physiology.

My work spans low-power analog and mixed-signal circuits, biosignal acquisition, and digital signal processing. At the University of Moratuwa, I contributed to biomedical IC research and student tapeout projects, including a dry-contact Ear-EEG system designed to monitor electrode–skin impedance and help reduce motion artifacts.

I enjoy moving between circuit-level details and the real-world needs a system should serve.
